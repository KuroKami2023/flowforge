# DESIGN.md — FlowForge landing

## Visual world (niche: forge control room)
Charcoal chrome (`#111418` family) against warm light canvas (`#f4f5f3`),
single molten-amber accent (`#f59e0b`). Inter body, system mono for node data.
Dark control-room bands (hero, final CTA) with amber mesh glow; light content
bands between. 8-16px radii, card-lift shadows.

## Landing composition
1. Slim top bar (64px) with forge mark and anchor links.
2. Dark full-bleed hero: headline with amber accent word, 17-word subtext,
   primary plus anchor CTAs, honest capability chips. Right: 3D-tilting
   operations photography collage with shine sweep and functional caption.
3. Glance band: three amber-dot facts (replaces hero micro-strip).
4. Node catalog rendered from live definitions, opened by a wide palette
   photograph with caption. Category groups keep small label cards (real data).
5. How-it-runs as numbered hairline rows with supporting photo (distinct from
   the catalog grid).
6. FAQ accordion, dark final CTA with forge mark, quiet footer.

## Motion (one authored moment)
Pointer-driven 3D tilt on the hero collage, amber orb drift, shine sweep,
floating overlap photo. Scroll reveal elsewhere. All gated by
`prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Hard rules honored
Zero em/en dashes. No kickers or eyebrows. No fake node-graph UI, no fake run
footers. Content-matched photography (server racks, data streams, automation)
with functional captions. One accent color locked.
